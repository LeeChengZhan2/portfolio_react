Outside work I'm mostly moving. Football, competitively, and long-distance
running — plus the gym in between, which exists mainly to keep the other two
possible.

## Running

Marathon distance is not really an athletic problem for most people who finish
one. It's an argument you have with yourself for several hours, and the training
is where you practise losing that argument gracefully. Turning up on a morning
you'd rather not is the entire skill; the fitness follows it around.

I've found it changes how I work more than how I look. A long run is the only
context where I reliably stop defending an approach I've already committed to,
which is why the fix for a problem I've been stuck on all week usually arrives
somewhere around kilometre fifteen.

## Football

Football is the opposite discipline, and I need both. Running is solitary and
paced; football is eleven people making decisions faster than they can explain
them, and being the person others can predict matters more than being the most
skilful one on the pitch. That is close to the truest description of working in a
small engineering team I know.

## Seven years of drums

Between 2013 and 2020 I played 24 Festival drums with JB Drum — over seventy
performances across Malaysia, Singapore and China, and eventually teaching
workshops to newer players.

Twenty-four drums in formation is a strange thing to have done before a career in
software, and a surprisingly good preparation for it. Nobody in the formation can
hear the whole piece from where they're standing. You hold your part, you trust
the timing, and if you drift you take everyone with you. Teaching it was harder
than playing it — the first time you have to explain something you only know in
your hands, you find out how much of it you actually understand.
