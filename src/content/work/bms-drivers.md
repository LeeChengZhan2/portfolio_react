I currently work as a software engineer in the Building Management System (BMS)
industry in Singapore, developing and debugging the software and drivers that let
a BMS platform talk to the equipment in a building — HVAC, lighting, fans.

The work is mostly about integration: making sure a platform and a piece of
hardware that were designed independently agree on what a message means, and
staying reliable once they do.
